How the work is built

How we work together

A consistent set of photographs for different channels.

Before planning a shot list, I spend time understanding how the company actually works: who carries its authority, where its culture becomes visible and which situations matter to customers, candidates and partners. The photography day should begin with decisions already made, not with guesses. The same discipline applies whether the subject is a corporate leadership team, a founder building a new venture, an artist positioning a body of work, or an individual building a personal brand.

1 · Understand

We begin with the organisation or individual, its goals, culture, audiences and the situations — corporate, entrepreneurial, artistic or personal — in which the visual presence must work.

2 · Position

We agree on how you should come across, then turn that into a clear visual direction — including wardrobe, studio or location choices, and grooming or styling support where required.

3 · Create

The shoot is planned as a usable set: close, half-body and full-body portraits; vertical and horizontal frames; clean executive and environmental images; editorial space for text where needed; and several looks or situations when the brief requires it.

4 · Roll out

The image system is adapted for websites, LinkedIn, speaker profiles, media kits, press and PR, presentations, recruitment, campaigns and international communication.

5 · Evolve

When leaders, teams or communication needs change, the visual language can be updated without starting again.

From your goals to a practical image library

I begin with identity, audience, market context, differentiation and the current gap between how the brand wants to be understood and how it is presently seen. Together we define the visual territory: level of formality, emotional distance, authority, accessibility, environment, colour, light and recurring motifs.

The photography then translates this positioning into a usable structured image set. Founder portraits, team situations, workplace details, products, editorial scenes and campaign images serve different channels, but communicate the same strategic position.

Project frameworkThe practical things worth agreeing before we start

What does the project fee cover?

Before I photograph anything, we put the scope in writing: preparation, the shoot, selection, retouching, the final fee and any real travel or production cost. Studio sessions in Vienna and Budapest have no travel surcharge. For a client office or another location, any travel and venue costs are stated separately in the estimate. The accepted written offer remains authoritative.

What arrives, and when?

You should not have to guess what happens after the shoot. The written offer sets the preview, selection and final-delivery dates, together with the file formats you actually need.

Project governance

Approval, confidentiality and publication

The written offer identifies who may approve the work, what remains private and where the finished images may be published.

One authorised approver

The commissioning organisation names one authorised approver. Projects with several stakeholders return one single consolidated feedback round rather than conflicting individual instructions.

Confidential by default

Unreleased portraits, internal events, strategy material, contact sheets and project discussions remain confidential by default. Access is limited to the agreed project team.

Publication requires permission

BANHALMI does not publish client images as a portfolio, case study, press item or social post without written permission. The approved channels and timing are recorded separately from the usage licence.

Internal use and third parties

Internal distribution within the commissioning organisation follows the accepted offer. Transfer to agencies, media partners or other third parties must fit the agreed licence or receive written approval.

File governance

Storage, access and deletion

The written offer records the delivery route, access period and any project-specific retention requirement. Delivery is not a promise of permanent archive storage.

Controlled access

Preview galleries, download links and working folders are limited to the agreed project team. Access links may expire and should not be forwarded outside the authorised group.

Delivered files and working files

The client receives the agreed final files. RAW captures, rejected frames, contact sheets and intermediate retouching files remain working material unless the written offer expressly includes them.

Retention and backup

The completed image set is delivered through the agreed cloud route. After the client confirms successful download, BANHALMI deletes the delivered client image files from its operational cloud/storage and does not retain a portfolio or archive copy. The client is therefore responsible for securely backing up the downloaded package. This file-deletion rule does not shorten statutory retention periods for invoices, contracts, accounting records, consent records or other legally required documentation. Delivery does not guarantee permanent storage by BANHALMI beyond the agreed access period.

Deletion requests

A deletion request can be submitted in writing. Files are removed where the request applies, except where continued retention is required for an accepted contract, accounting, legal claims or another valid legal basis.

Publication control

Image use, approvals and reference rights

The written offer defines the intended channels, decision-makers and any restrictions before production. Delivery of files does not by itself authorise BANHALMI to publish a project as a reference.

Agreed use

The client may use the delivered images within the licence recorded in the offer, including the agreed internal, press, employer-branding, social or campaign channels. New uses outside that scope should be cleared in writing.

People and permissions

The client identifies who may be photographed and is responsible for the organisational notices, participant permissions and internal approvals required for the planned use. Sensitive or restricted participants can be excluded before or during production.

Approval before release

Where approval is required, only the selected and approved final images are released for publication. Contact sheets, previews and unapproved variants are not publication files.

Portfolio and later restrictions

BANHALMI does not use commissioned client images in its portfolio, awards, advertising, press, social media, teaching or other promotional material without separate explicit client permission. Any such permission is separate from the client's usage licence and can define the approved images, channels and timing. Later restriction or removal request for reference use can be sent in writing to the studio contact after publication.

What if the date changes?

Once the offer is accepted and any agreed booking payment is made, the date is yours. If plans move, the accepted offer already tells us how rescheduling, cancellation and documented outside costs are handled.
