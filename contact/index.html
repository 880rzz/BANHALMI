Project coordination

The right team for the assignment

Once your request arrives, I review the practical requirements: location, timing, number of people, intended use and the level of discretion.

When the assignment needs more than one person, I bring in trusted photographers, retouchers or coordinators from my Vienna–Budapest network and explain clearly who is responsible for what.

Your point of contact is defined after I review the request. For enquiries related to AmCham Austria, Viko Speier is the designated corporate contact for Bánhalmi Norbert e.U.

AmCham Austria membership & business network

Bánhalmi Norbert e.U. is a member of AmCham Austria, a high-quality international business network supporting Austrian–United States commercial relations.

We support selected AmCham Austria and member-community events with professional photography and visual documentation.
