Budapest studio leadership

Speier coordinates the day-to-day professional presence of the BANHALMI Budapest studio. She supports project preparation, client communication, creative production and selected photography assignments, helping ensure a consistent experience between the Vienna and Budapest bases.

Her background combines portrait and fashion photography with business, marketing and creative-strategy experience. This makes her especially well suited to projects where personal presence, brand positioning and practical production need to work together.

A collaboration built since 2018

The professional relationship began in 2018 and has developed into a long-term creative partnership. Speier contributes a distinctive visual sensitivity, reliable project coordination and a strong understanding of how portraits support professional credibility and personal brands.

AmCham Austria

AmCham Austria representation

Bánhalmi Norbert e.U. is a member of AmCham Austria. Within this high-quality international business network, Speier Viko is the designated liaison who represents our company and helps maintain relationships with the community and its members.

BANHALMI also supports selected AmCham Austria and member-community events through professional photography and visual documentation.
