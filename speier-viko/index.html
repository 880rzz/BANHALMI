Speier Viko — photographer and creative strategist

An award-winning photographer and creative strategist whose work connects executive presence, human insight and brand positioning. She directs the BANHALMI Budapest Studio and serves as the company’s contact within the AmCham Austria community.

Professional overview

Executive profile

Speier Viko combines professional portraiture with economics, marketing research and agency strategy. Her multidisciplinary background enables her to understand not only how a person should look in an image, but what that image needs to communicate to clients, teams and international audiences.

A profile built across business and creativity

She began her career in economics and an international corporate environment, gaining a structured understanding of organisations, risk and decision-making. She later completed professional photography studies and a master’s degree in marketing, building an uncommon bridge between visual craft, commercial strategy and client experience.

Strategy behind the image

As a strategic planner at Mito Creative, she worked on communication, employer-branding and campaign projects connected to brands including Magyar Telekom, Burger King and Mondelez. This experience shapes her photographic work today: every assignment begins with purpose, audience and positioning before visual execution.

International recognition

In 2024, together with art director Adél Czebe, she won the Hungarian Young Lions competition in the PR category and represented Hungary and Mito Group at the international Young Lions competition in Cannes. The achievement positioned her at the intersection of creative strategy, social insight and internationally competitive communication.

Photography with executive relevance

Her work spans executive and personal-brand portraits, fashion, beauty and advertising campaigns. She creates calm, collaborative environments in which leaders, founders and creative professionals can appear credible, contemporary and unmistakably themselves. Technical precision is paired with empathy and a clear understanding of brand context.

International practice and professional leadership

Her professional development includes intensive fashion and beauty photography training in New York with Lindsay Adler. As an OM SYSTEM Ambassador, she contributes to professional education, camera demonstrations and mentoring. At the BANHALMI Budapest Studio, she leads local client relationships, productions and creative coordination.

Purpose beyond commercial work

Her practice also includes socially engaged projects. Her first solo exhibition supported the Hungarian Charity Service of the Order of Malta, and later charity initiatives connected photography with fundraising for emergency and child-rescue causes. This commitment reinforces a leadership profile grounded in responsibility as well as creative excellence.
