FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for people who want clarity before they step in front of the camera.

Portraits & preparation

Who is executive portrait photography for?

For founders, C-level leaders, consultants, experts and teams whose image needs to create trust before the first conversation.

What is the difference between a headshot and an executive portrait?

A headshot mainly identifies the person. An executive portrait also communicates role, authority, approachability and brand context.

How should I prepare for the session?

You receive practical guidance on wardrobe, grooming, image goals and intended usage before the session.

Can you help with posing?

Yes. Direction is calm and precise, with attention to posture, expression, hands and the way the image will be used.

Production & locations

Do you work on location?

Yes. Sessions can take place in the Vienna or Budapest studio, at your office, at an event venue or at another agreed location.

Can you provide styling, hair and make-up?

Yes. A stylist, hair professional and make-up artist can be arranged when the project benefits from them.

Can several people be photographed in one production?

Yes. Executive teams, boards, founders and departments can be photographed with a consistent visual system.

Can you provide more than one photographer?

Yes. Larger productions and C-level events can be covered by a coordinated multi-photographer team.

Retouching, delivery & files

Is on-site immediate retouching available?

Yes. For selected executive, event and press productions, images can be selected, professionally retouched and delivered on location during the assignment.

Can you deliver print-ready files?

Yes. On request, final images are delivered in print-ready resolution and colour profile, alongside web-optimised versions for digital use.

How are the final images selected?

Selection can be guided together during or after the session, depending on the production format.

How quickly are images delivered?

Delivery timing is defined in the written offer. Accelerated and same-day workflows can be arranged for selected projects.

Brand, events & artistic commissions

What does visual brand positioning include?

It aligns image language, portrait style, visual hierarchy and usage across website, LinkedIn, press, campaigns and institutional communication.

Do you photograph C-level events?

Yes. Executive meetings, conferences, leadership events, award ceremonies and high-level institutional occasions are documented discreetly.

Is fine-art or intimate photography possible?

Yes, only within a respectful artistic framework, with clear consent, privacy and boundaries agreed in advance.

Pricing, rights & administration

Is the calculated price final?

No. It is a non-binding orientation estimate. The final written offer depends on scope, location, timing, usage rights, production complexity and tax status.

How are usage rights handled?

Usage rights depend on the project, media, duration and territory and are confirmed in the written offer.

Do you support EU reverse charge?

Yes. Eligible EU businesses outside Austria may receive a reverse-charge estimate after business and VAT details are provided and verified.

What happens to my images under GDPR?

Images and personal data are handled confidentially and only for the agreed purpose. Publication takes place only with permission.
