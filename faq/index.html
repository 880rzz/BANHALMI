FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

It is normal to have practical questions before a photography project. Here are the answers—plain and simple.

Is this a one-off shoot or can we work together over time?

It can be a one-off shoot. If you need portraits for several leaders, images for several channels or a library that stays current, we can also work together over time. We begin with what you genuinely need.

Portraits & preparation

Who is a leadership portrait for?

For founders, leaders, consultants, experts and anyone a client is likely to meet in a photograph before meeting in person.

What is the difference between a headshot and a leadership portrait?

A headshot shows clearly who you are. A leadership portrait says more about your personality, your role and the way you want to meet the world.

How should I prepare for the session?

You receive practical guidance on wardrobe, grooming, image goals and intended usage before the session.

Can you help with posing?

Yes. Direction is calm and precise, with attention to posture, expression, hands and the way the image will be used.

Production & locations

Do you work on location?

Yes. Sessions can take place in the Vienna or Budapest studio, at your office, at an event venue or at another agreed location.

Can you provide styling, hair and make-up?

Yes. A stylist, hair professional and make-up artist can be arranged when the project benefits from them.

Can several people be photographed in one production?

Yes. Executive teams, boards, founders and departments can be photographed with a consistent visual system.

Can you provide more than one photographer?

Yes. Larger productions and C-level events can be covered by a coordinated multi-photographer team.

Retouching, delivery & files

Is on-site immediate retouching available?

Yes. For selected executive, event and press productions, images can be selected, professionally retouched and delivered on location during the assignment.

Can you deliver print-ready files?

Yes. On request, final images are delivered in print-ready resolution and colour profile, alongside web-optimised versions for digital use.

How are the final images selected?

Selection can be guided together during or after the session, depending on the production format.

How quickly are images delivered?

Delivery timing is defined in the written offer. Accelerated and same-day workflows can be arranged for selected projects.

Brand, events & artistic commissions

What does visual brand positioning include?

It aligns image language, portrait style, visual hierarchy and usage across website, LinkedIn, press, campaigns and institutional communication.

Do you photograph C-level events?

Yes. Executive meetings, conferences, leadership events, award ceremonies and high-level institutional occasions are documented discreetly.

Is fine-art or intimate photography possible?

Yes, only within a respectful artistic framework, with clear consent, privacy and boundaries agreed in advance.

Pricing, rights & administration

Is the calculated price final?

No. It is a non-binding preliminary estimate. The final written offer depends on scope, location, timing, usage rights, production complexity and tax status.

How are usage rights handled?

Usage rights depend on the project, media, duration and territory and are confirmed in the written offer.

Do you support EU reverse charge?

Yes. Eligible EU businesses outside Austria may receive a reverse-charge estimate after business and VAT details are provided and verified.

What happens to my images under GDPR?

Images and personal data are handled confidentially and only for the agreed purpose. Publication takes place only with permission.

What the project fee covers

The calculator provides a non-binding estimate. The final scope, fee and delivery schedule become binding only in the accepted written offer.

Defined production scope

Preparation, photography, agreed selection and retouching are listed separately in the written offer.

Commercial usage included

The standard licence is unlimited in time and territory for the commissioning organisation and the agreed communication channels. Copyright remains with the photographer.

Vienna and Budapest

On-site work in Vienna and Budapest is included. Travel or production costs outside these locations are confirmed before booking.

Transparent totals

The quote shows net amount, VAT where applicable and gross total. Additional work is never added without written agreement.

Delivery system

What arrives, when it arrives and how it can be used

The accepted written offer records the preview deadline, the client-selection deadline and the final-delivery deadline. This keeps timing visible before production begins instead of leaving it to assumptions.

Selection and approval

A structured proof gallery is delivered for selection where the project requires client choice. The number of final images, approval rounds and decision-makers are fixed in the written scope.

Final image package

Approved photographs are supplied as high-resolution master files and web-ready versions. Agreed crops for LinkedIn, press, website, presentations or campaigns are included only when listed in the offer.

Retouching and file structure

Retouching follows the agreed visual standard. Files use a clear naming structure, and delivery notes identify master files, web files and platform-specific variants.

Timing and follow-up

Preview, selection and final-delivery dates are project-specific and written into the accepted offer. Urgent or same-day delivery is available only when agreed before production.

File governance

Storage, access and deletion

The written offer records the delivery route, access period and any project-specific retention requirement. Delivery is not a promise of permanent archive storage.

Controlled access

Preview galleries, download links and working folders are limited to the agreed project team. Access links may expire and should not be forwarded outside the authorised group.

Delivered files and working files

The client receives the agreed final files. RAW captures, rejected frames, contact sheets and intermediate retouching files remain working material unless the written offer expressly includes them.

Retention and backup

BANHALMI may keep project files for a reasonable operational period, but does not guarantee permanent storage. The client is responsible for securely backing up the delivered package after download.

Deletion requests

A deletion request can be submitted in writing. Files are removed where the request applies, except where continued retention is required for an accepted contract, accounting, legal claims or another valid legal basis.

Publication control

Image use, approvals and reference rights

The written offer defines the intended channels, decision-makers and any restrictions before production. Delivery of files does not by itself authorise BANHALMI to publish a project as a reference.

Agreed use

The client may use the delivered images within the licence recorded in the offer, including the agreed internal, press, employer-branding, social or campaign channels. New uses outside that scope should be cleared in writing.

People and permissions

The client identifies who may be photographed and is responsible for the organisational notices, participant permissions and internal approvals required for the planned use. Sensitive or restricted participants can be excluded before or during production.

Approval before release

Where approval is required, only the selected and approved final images are released for publication. Contact sheets, previews and unapproved variants are not publication files.

Portfolio and later restrictions

BANHALMI uses client work in its portfolio, awards, press or educational material only where that use is agreed or otherwise lawfully permitted. A later restriction or removal request can be submitted in writing and will be assessed against existing licences, publications and legal obligations.

Commercial clarity

Booking fees, invoices and payment

The accepted written offer records the project fee, currency, VAT treatment, invoicing schedule and any agreed booking fee. The invoice states the applicable payment deadline.

When a booking fee applies

A booking fee is payable only when it is stated in the accepted offer. It secures the agreed production capacity and is credited against the project fee unless the offer states otherwise.

Invoices and currency

Invoices show the agreed currency, net amount, applicable VAT and gross total. Any staged or final invoice follows the milestones recorded in the accepted offer.

Payment and delivery

Payment is made by the method and deadline shown on the invoice. Where an agreed payment is overdue, further production, final delivery or renewed access may be paused until the account is settled.

No unapproved charges

Additional work, third-party costs or scope changes are invoiced only after written approval. Statutory or expressly agreed consequences of late payment remain unaffected.

Project governance

Approval, confidentiality and publication

The written offer identifies who may approve the work, what remains private and where the finished images may be published.

One authorised approver

The commissioning organisation names one authorised approver. Projects with several stakeholders return one single consolidated feedback round rather than conflicting individual instructions.

Confidential by default

Unreleased portraits, internal events, strategy material, contact sheets and project discussions remain confidential by default. Access is limited to the agreed project team.

Publication requires permission

BANHALMI does not publish client images as a portfolio, case study, press item or social post without written permission. The approved channels and timing are recorded separately from the usage licence.

Internal use and third parties

Internal distribution within the commissioning organisation follows the accepted offer. Transfer to agencies, media partners or other third parties must fit the agreed licence or receive written approval.

Scheduling and continuity

Booking, rescheduling and contingency

The written offer confirms when a date becomes reserved, how changes are handled and which costs can arise if circumstances move after production has started.

Confirmed reservation

A date is reserved only after written acceptance and any agreed booking payment. An enquiry, calculator result or calendar conversation alone does not hold a production date.

Rescheduling and delay

Changes caused by executive availability, venue access, weather or programme timing are handled in writing. We first seek a proportionate replacement date; confirmed external costs remain visible.

Cancellation and external costs

Cancellation terms are stated in the individual offer. Work already completed and documented third-party costs may remain payable; no undefined penalty is added outside the accepted agreement.

Illness, failure and force majeure

If illness, essential equipment failure or an event beyond reasonable control prevents delivery, BANHALMI prioritises a replacement date, equivalent professional solution or fair adjustment of the affected scope.
