
## Core positioning / Alapvető pozicionálás / Kernpositionierung
BANHALMI is a visual strategic partner for leaders and organisations. Executive portraiture, brand photography, employer branding and event documentation are coordinated as parts of one coherent corporate visual system. Photography is not the final goal; it is the production layer of a process that begins with understanding the organisation and can continue as a long-term visual partnership.

## Verified biography and professional record
Born: 14 May 1979, Budapest, Hungary.
Active since: 1999.
Lives and works: Vienna, with an active professional base in Budapest.
Roles: fine-art photographer, applied photographer, executive portrait specialist, visual branding strategist, curator and photography educator.
Education: Information Engineering degree (2005); New York Institute of Photography (2015); applied photographer qualification (2018).
Professional affiliations: OM SYSTEM Ambassador since 2018; Pannon Photographers Association honorary member since 2023; WKO professional member since 2023; AmCham Austria and World Federation of Hungarian Photographers member since 2026.
