# BANHALMI voice standard — EN / HU / DE-AT
Status: editorial working standard. Source: selected outgoing, pre-2026 messages written by Norbert Bánhalmi; do not copy private correspondence into public code.

## Core voice
- First person when speaking as the photographer; plural only when discussing actual team delivery.
- Begin with the person and the practical request, not brand superlatives.
- Warm, attentive, direct and specific; professional without corporate jargon.
- Explain choices and offer a tailored alternative when a standard package is not a fit.
- Say what will happen, what needs to be clarified, and what the customer can expect.
- Avoid invented guarantees, artificial scarcity, generic luxury claims, excessive superlatives and hard-selling CTAs.
- Never reproduce customer names, private details or correspondence without explicit permission.

## Hungarian
- Natural polite address for first-contact premium clients; familiar form only when the relationship warrants it.
- Prefer short, direct sentences: what the client needs, how the photography can work, what is included, what remains to be agreed.
- Avoid machine-like compounds, repetitive SEO strings and bureaucratic phrasing.

## English
- Clear international business English with courteous, personal tone.
- Prefer concrete verbs and plain language over abstract agency jargon.
- Do not imitate historical grammar or spelling mistakes in old correspondence.

## German (Austria)
- Natural Austrian professional German, polite Sie, direct but not stiff.
- Keep service scope and usage rights understandable; avoid literal Hungarian syntax.
- Historical sample coverage in German is limited: native editorial QA required before treating this as Norbert's verified German idiolect.

## Search and trust contract
- Preserve verified Vienna/Wien/Bécs and Budapest intent, canonical, hreflang, title/H1, schema and image license metadata.
- SEO/GEO terms belong where relevant, not in every sentence.
- Claims of personal creative direction, team size, memberships, studios and response/delivery time require current factual support.
- Customer emails are internal research, not testimonials, references, AI training data or publishable evidence by default.
- Never turn an individual usage-rights grant from email into a universal licensing policy.
- GDPR: no private customer names, addresses, quotes, email bodies, or other personal data in public repo or structured data.

## Editorial checklist
1. Would Norbert naturally say this to a client?
2. Does it answer a real buyer question?
3. Is it accurate and legally supportable?
4. Does it read naturally in the target language?
5. Are the three languages equivalent in substance, without literal translation?
6. Does it preserve the visual hierarchy, schema, privacy and existing galleries?
